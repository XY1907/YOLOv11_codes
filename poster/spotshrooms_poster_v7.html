The Problem

Across Asia, mushroom farms still rely on manual monitoring. Workers cannot watch every shelf around the clock, so mature mushrooms get over-watered and rot — a severe, avoidable harvest loss for smallholder oyster-mushroom farms.

Novelty: uniquely fuses growth-stage vision with an ML-predicted misting duration — replacing one-size-fits-all timers with a crop-aware, sensor-driven irrigation loop that runs as a retrofit add-on on a single Raspberry Pi.

Two Cooperating AI Models

① YOLOv8 — Computer Vision

Trained on real images from a Vietnam oyster-mushroom farm shelf. Detects the growth stage of every cluster and counts mushrooms present.

Three growth classes
No Sprout Small & Medium Mature
② Random Forest — Misting Regressor

Trained on synthetic data grounded in oyster-cultivation literature. Predicts the exact misting duration (seconds) from live conditions.

Inputs
HumidityTemperatureCO₂ Growth stage
Safety guardrails override the model: never mist Mature · stop if Humidity > 92% · ventilate if CO₂ > 1500 ppm.
